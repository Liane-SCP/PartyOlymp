Modifications for this game:
- Converted the original Blender 2.69 file to glTF 2.0 / GLB.
- Rebuilt legacy Blender Internal materials as metallic-roughness PBR using
  the original packed diffuse and normal textures.
- Derived an eye-emission mask from the author's painted red eye pixels.
- Applied the original Mirror modifier and smoothed vertex normals.
- Preserved the original weighted skeleton and fourteen authored character
  animation clips; removed only presentation camera and light animations.
- Partitioned authored crown and chest triangles into removable skinned armor;
  added a recessed inner surface and a luminous chest core for battle phases.
